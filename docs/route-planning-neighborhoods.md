# Neighborhoods in future run plans

Give every future street target a `neighborhood` label. Show it on the run card/sidebar and add it to the Google Calendar title and description, while keeping a numbered street address in Location. Match the selected street section, not a different disconnected namesake. Use an honest landmark-area label when a formal neighborhood boundary is uncertain.

Current planning labels:

- Gladstone Road: Richland.
- Rodmor Road: Pilgrim Gardens.
- Grove Place: Grange Estate area.
- Wilson Drive: Hillcrest / Llanerch Hills edge (the target crosses Township Line Road).
- Walnut Place and Covington Road: Manoa / Grange Estate area.

Keep Llanerch, Llanerch Hills, Pilgrim Gardens, Richland, Lynnewood and Hillcrest separate when choosing future unfinished streets. “Lanark” and “Linwood” in the conversation are interpreted as Llanerch and Lynnewood; do not invent municipal boundary polygons from those spellings. Neighborhood names are human-friendly planning labels, not official ZIP or CityStrides boundaries. Preserve the existing 19083-first priority.

Hillcrest Elementary School is at 2601 Bond Avenue, Drexel Hill, PA 19026. The surrounding planning label is Hillcrest / Drexel Hill, distinct from Llanerch Hills to its west. School attendance areas are not neighborhood boundaries.
