Distances are Google walking directions to the numbered addresses, not total run distances. The October 28 proximity audit finds 5.0% of Gladstone, 5.3% of Fairlamb and 33.5% of Chestnut samples within 25 m of recorded running GPS tracks. Proximity does not establish CityStrides completion. Exeter, Dover and southern Coopertown have no samples within that distance.

`targets.json` is the plan; `new-street-evidence.json` records geometry and Google walking evidence; `calendar-verified.json` records verified events. `new-tracks.geojson` carries the latest recorded runs into the running-only map and is deduplicated by activity ID.
